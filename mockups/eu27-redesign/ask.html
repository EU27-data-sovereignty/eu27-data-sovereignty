Who keeps Germany’s Central Register of Foreign Nationals?

The Federal Office for Migration and Refugees (BAMF) keeps the register as the register authority. The Federal Office of Administration (BVA) processes the stored data on BAMF’s behalf and under its instructions.[2]

The register consists of a general data stock and a separately kept visa file.[2] The findings do not yet say where its infrastructure runs.

[2] § 1 AZR-Gesetz, gesetze-im-internet.de · T1 official law portal · Standard
